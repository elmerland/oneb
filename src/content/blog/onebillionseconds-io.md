Years ago I watched Neil deGrasse Tyson [explain](https://youtu.be/YPenDUY68rM?t=302) how long one billion seconds actually is. It stuck with me.

It is longer than it sounds — 31.7 years.

When I was getting close to my own milestone, I decided to make my own at [onebillionseconds.io](https://www.onebillionseconds.io/)! It opens with numbers that put life in perspective, then lets the numbers get personal.
