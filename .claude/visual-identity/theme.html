ONEBILLIONSECONDS.IO

2024 · 03 · 14

A long time ago I watched a video by Neil deGrasse Tyson about how long one billion seconds is. The video stayed with me for some reason.

I recently hit my one billion seconds milestone and saw that nobody had made a website for this yet — so here is my attempt at a one billion second calculator. You put in your birthday, you get a date. That's the whole thing.

It is the kind of website I love most: one input, one number, one idea. No tracking, no newsletter, no roadmap. Just a small thing in the world.

MILESTONES NOBODY KNOWS ABOUT

2024 · 02 · 02

There is a particular pleasure in noticing a number flip over that means nothing to anyone else. The odometer on the way to work. A photo timestamped one year to the minute from another. Your one-billionth second.

Part of what I like about it is that the universe didn't notice either. You are alone with the fact for a moment. It feels like being let in on something.

THE CASE FOR SINGLE-PURPOSE WEBSITES

2024 · 01 · 18

I have a soft spot for websites that do exactly one thing. ismycomputeron.com. cantyousleep.com. The pages that solve precisely the problem in their URL and then stop.

They are not platforms. They are not products. They are just there, like a hand-painted sign in a small town.

Every time I make one, I am reminded that the web is still a good place to leave a small note for strangers.
